Hisaab - Calculator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Hisaab - Calculator ("the app") is a calculator for Android.

What the app does with your data

  • The app calculates on your phone. The text you select and the numbers in it are processed on your device only.
  • The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.
  • The app does not request internet access and does not contain ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your phone

  • Your calculation history is saved only on your device so you can see it again. It is not uploaded and is excluded from cloud backup.
  • You can delete one entry or all history inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. When you tap Calculate or Replace, the app reads only the text you selected and can copy the result to your clipboard at your request.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will appear on this page.
